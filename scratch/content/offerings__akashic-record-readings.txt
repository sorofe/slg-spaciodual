Akasha is Universal life force and the creative mind that is unlimited space unbound by any element in nature.
The Akashic Records
or
The Souls Records
are an etheric Library of Information that contains knowledge and intelligence about every sentient being in the universe. It contains all the soul’s lifetimes, origins, emotions and past. Your Akashic Record is the divine knowing of the entire experience of your soul. Whether you are a young or ancient soul, the Akashic Record holds the energetic imprint of your thoughts, feelings, memories, actions, and deeds experienced in each lifetime. This accumulation of energy creates the lessons and experiences necessary in each successive lifetime that assist in our evolution.
The Akashic Records are a compendium of consciousness that the mind uses to illustrate the understanding that every thought, word, emotion, experience and intent ever to have occurred in the past, present, or future in any dimension, planet or timeline exists in the collective consciousness that is accessible to us all on some level. A reading is a deep remembrance of who you are at soul level and an activation for your path ahead, in line with your purpose and gifts. A reading filters through the etheric book of your souls memories, beyond time and space and holds the wisdom of who you are at soul origin, in purest form.
Therefore, it is a great source of information if you want to find out more about your life purpose, past, and soul gifts. Your soul—the essence of who you are—has often traveled through many lifetimes to make you who you are today. By finding out more about your soul’s journey, you can better understand, heal, and thrive in this current life.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is this a Past Life Reading?
An Akashic Reading can source information from and for pretty much any topic including past lives, cosmic experiences and genetic memories.
What kind of questions, should I ask?
Imagine you have the opportunity to speak with your higher self; the higher self that knows and loves you intimately, unconditionally without judgement and wants to reveal to you all the ways in which you are powerful and perfect. What would you want to know, how would you personalize your prayer?
Set aside some quiet time for self - inquiry. Light a candle, take a bath, eat a good meal, go out in nature, laugh or cry and ask yourself what information do I truly desire to know that can effect my life in a profound and purposeful way. Craft and create 3 questions that are specific, focused, direct burning desire to know kind of questions.
How long is the session?
Sessions usually run about 2 hours.
Can I record the session?
In-Person session can be recorded by you or your practitioner. Please be sure to request a recording before your session begins. All virtual sessions are recorded via Zoom and sent to you within 24hrs.
