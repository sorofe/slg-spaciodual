Reiki
is an energetic practice that draws from the infinite wellspring of healing that is available in the liminal realm.
Energy therapy, energy healing, vibrational medicine, psychic healing, spiritual medicine or spiritual healing are branches of alternative medicine based on energy work through the belief that healers can channel life force healing energy into a client and effect positive results. Reiki Healing is a multidimensional principle that as a whole, opens an energetic gateway that enhances the experience of everything your soul chooses. Reiki empowers you with an effective method to facilitate healing for self and others.
Energy can stagnate in the body where there has been physical injury or emotional distress. In time, these energy blocks can reveal themselves as illness, anxiety, depression, fatigue or disease. Reiki healing as a holistic practice accesses and activates the body's subtle energy systems (aura, chakras, etheric body) to remove blocks and encourage powerful alignment and flow.
By removing/adjusting or acknowledging mental and emotional distortion and allowing the energy to move through and bring healing, we restore balance and the body's inherent ability to heal itself is stimulated.
Through the use of ancient symbols and universal life force we use reiki to purify through time and space, becoming more finely tuned vessels and deepening our connection to our own spiritual nature and gifts.
