Chakra
is the Sanskrit word for wheel. Chakra balancing is based on the ancient Eastern belief in a series of seven chakras, or energy centers in the physical body.
The 7 chakras are the 7 wheels of energy in our body. These energy centers are located at specific points ranging from the feet to the top of the head.
The physical, emotional and spiritual health of your body is dependent on free flowing energy within the body. The chakras are the passageway for that energy to flow and no chakra works independently of the other chakras as they are all part of the whole energy system. Each chakra only works fully when the other chakras are also fully engaged.
As above, so below, as within, so without.
Chakra Balancing healing sessions are an excellent way to heal your energy flow and find ease and grace within your body, mind and spirit through the creation or reinstatement of harmony and balance with your field.
